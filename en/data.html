Looking for datasets?

In order to help the scientific and/or teaching community, we have made the choice to make the datasets we use available. These datasets are hosted on the Mendeley Data platform, and documented through articles in the open access journal Data in Brief.

Data were produced by Atmo Normandie, an association in charge of monitoring air quality in Normandy, France.
  • new! Fixed and mobile low-cost sensor measurements (NO2/PM), 10 months.
  • Fixed and mobile low-cost sensor measurements (PM), 1.5 months.
  • Deployed fixed low-cost sensor measurements, 1 year.
  • Collocated low-cost sensors, 3 months.
